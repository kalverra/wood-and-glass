# Questions or a custom idea?

Have a question about a piece, want to ask about availability, or have an idea for something made just for you?

The easiest way to get in touch right now is through the Wood and Glass LLC Etsy shop. Open the shop and choose **Contact** to send Dave a message.

[Visit Wood and Glass LLC on Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/WoodAndGlassLLCShop)

You can also meet Wood and Glass LLC in person at craft shows. Check the [upcoming events](/#upcoming-events) section on the home page for the next scheduled show.
