# Made by hand, one piece at a time

Wood and Glass LLC is owned and operated by Dave Hamrick, a disabled U.S. Navy submarine veteran.

The workshop is where Dave continues to build and create. Working with wood and glass gives him the opportunity to turn carefully chosen materials into useful, distinctive pieces. Each project is made with attention to the grain, color, character, and finish of the material.

The work includes turned wooden writing instruments, cutting and charcuterie boards, and handcrafted glass pieces. No two pieces of natural wood are exactly alike, and that individuality is part of what makes handmade work special.

Wood and Glass LLC is a small, veteran-owned Virginia business. You can find the work online through the Etsy shop and in person at craft shows. Custom ideas and questions are welcome.
